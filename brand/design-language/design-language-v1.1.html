Unbounded matches the Bitesized wordmark, so headlines feel on-brand. Inter is the most readable heavy font for captions on a phone. Mono labels give the "real system" feel. The script accent echoes the Back in a Gist logo, for playful asides only.

3

Layout & safe zones

One 1080×1920 master that works on Shorts, Reels and TikTok

ZoneSpec
Safe areax 64–930 y 250–1470: clear of the TikTok, Reels and Shorts UI (the strictest of the three)
Progress barTop of safe area, 866×10, orange fill runs linearly for the whole video
Corner mark84px, top-left under the progress bar, on every charcoal scene. Hidden during the branding sting
HeadlineMost scenes, not all. ≤5 words, ≤2 lines, one ember word. Skip it on big-visual moments (a full diagram, the gist card) so the video never feels like a slideshow. Max 3 headline scenes in a row
StageDiagrams and visuals. Flows run top → bottom (vertical suits 9:16)
CaptionsAlways the same place. 3–5 words per chunk, current word in ember

The optional headline and the captions never say the same thing: the headline names the idea and the captions carry the voiceover.
