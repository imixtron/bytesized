# Pronunciation tracker

Tech terms the voiceover says wrong (or might). Add them **as they come up**: when `/script` writes a new term or a render sounds off. Once there are a few, they go into an ElevenLabs pronunciation dictionary that `npm run voice` applies to every episode.
